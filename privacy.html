Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This page explains how formentineric.github.io (the "site") handles personal data, in accordance with Article 13 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation (EU) 2016/679) and the Italian Privacy Code (D.Lgs. 196/2003).

1. Data controller

Eric Formentin, private individual, Italy.
Contact: formentinec@gmail.com

2. Summary

  • No cookies of any kind are set by this site.
  • No advertising, profiling or cross-site tracking.
  • Fonts, icons and scripts are hosted on this site itself: your browser does not contact Google Fonts or other content delivery networks.
  • The only external service is a cookieless, EU-hosted visit counter (GoatCounter), which is not loaded at all if your browser sends a "Global Privacy Control" or "Do Not Track" signal.

3. Hosting and server logs

The site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. (USA). When you visit any GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. This processing is necessary to deliver the site and protect it from abuse (legal basis: legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: GitHub General Privacy Statement.

4. Visit statistics (GoatCounter)

To know how many people visit the site, I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter that only produces aggregate statistics (pages viewed, referring site, browser, operating system, screen size, language, approximate country).

  • It does not set cookies and does not store identifiers in your browser.
  • Your IP address and full browser signature are not stored: they are used only in memory (for up to 8 hours) to avoid counting the same visit twice, and are replaced by a random string.
  • Data are stored on servers of Hetzner Online GmbH in Finland and Germany (EU). They are not shared with third parties and are not combined with other data.
  • The counter script only reads an optional opt-out flag in your browser's local storage.

Legal basis: legitimate interest in knowing aggregate usage of the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Because the tool only produces aggregate, non-identifying statistics for this single site, no consent banner is required under the Italian Data Protection Authority's guidelines on cookies and other tracking tools (10 June 2021).

How to opt out: enable "Global Privacy Control" or "Do Not Track" in your browser, or open this link once: the counter will stop counting your visits in this browser.

5. Emails you send me

If you contact me by email, I use your email address and the content of your message only to reply to you (legal basis: steps taken at your request and legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). These data are not shared with third parties and are kept only as long as needed for our correspondence.

6. External links

The site links to external websites (e.g. LinkedIn, GitHub). Those sites are only contacted if you click the link, and they have their own privacy policies.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to their processing, and to data portability (Articles 15–22 GDPR). To exercise these rights, write to the email address in section 1.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or with the supervisory authority of the EU country where you live or work.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated if the site changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
